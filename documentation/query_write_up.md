Query 1 — Order Detail Join

This query combines the ORDERS, CUSTOMERS, and PRODUCTS tables to provide a complete view of each order. It shows the customer name, product name, category, quantity, unit price, and calculated line revenue. The line revenue is calculated by multiplying the quantity ordered by the product unit price. This is important because it brings information from the different tables together and provides a detailed view of individual sales transactions.

Query 2 — Total Revenue per Customer

This query calculates the total revenue generated by each customer by adding the revenue from all of their orders. It groups the orders by customer and calculates total revenue using the quantity and product price. This is important because it helps the business understand how much revenue each customer contributes and identify customers with higher levels of spending.

Query 3 — Total Revenue per Product Category

This query calculates the total revenue generated by each product category. It joins the ORDERS and PRODUCTS tables, calculates revenue for each order, and groups the results by product category. This is important because it allows the business to compare the performance of different product categories and identify which categories generate the most revenue.

Query 4 — Top 5 Customers by Total Spend

This query identifies the five customers with the highest total spend. It calculates the total revenue for each customer, sorts the results from highest to lowest, and returns only the top five customers. This is important because it helps the business identify its highest-value customers, who may be important for customer retention and future sales strategies.
